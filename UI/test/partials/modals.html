Photo & Halftoning Configuration Guide

Thermal receipt printers are 1-bit monochrome devices: each micro-pin can only burn solid black or leave paper white. TinyPOS uses advanced digital image processing to reproduce photographic quality:

Dithering Algorithms
  • Floyd-Steinberg (Default): Error-diffusion algorithm with continuous micro-dot distribution. Best for soft skin tones and smooth gradients.
  • Atkinson: Apple Macintosh classic. Diffuses 75% of error and drops 25%, keeping highlights pure white and preventing dark ink pooling.
  • Bayer 8x8 Matrix: Ordered threshold grid producing a retro comic book and newspaper halftone pattern.
Edge Sharpening (Unsharp Mask)

Thermal printer pins bleed heat into adjacent paper fibers ("dot gain").

Applying an Unsharp Mask filter before halftoning accentuates high-frequency edges — making eyes, hair strands, teeth, and fine textures razor-sharp on thermal paper!

Quality Presets
• Portrait / Face: Lifts midtone shadows so cheeks and eyes don't become muddy black holes.
• Crisp & Detailed: Maximum unsharp masking; ideal for jewelry, hair, and complex textures.
• Balanced: Natural dynamic range for landscapes, travel, and everyday snaps.
• High Contrast: Punchy blacks and stark whites for sketches, silhouettes, and logos.
• Retro Halftone: Structured dot matrix look reminiscent of vintage newsprint.
